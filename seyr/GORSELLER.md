# Seyr — Ana Karakter Görselleri

Minyatür tarzı, erkek yolcu. 10 görünüm (her 10 seviyede bir unvan):
**Garip → Yolcu → Er → Alp → Bahadır → Ulu → Bilge → Kutlu → Nurlu → Simurg**

## Kurallar
1. **Önce sadece 1. görünümü üret.** Beğenince her yeni görünümde **bir öncekinin görselini yükle**: yüz ve vücut aynı kalsın.
2. Hepsi **aynı sohbette**, aynı stil cümlesiyle.
3. **Arka plan düz magenta** (#E8087F). Karakterde pembe/magenta renk olmasın; temizlerken karışır.
4. **Yazı, harf, hat yok.** Yapay zeka bozuk yazar; ayrıca oyunda "el gizli" kalacak.
5. Ayaklar dahil **boydan**, ortada, her görselde **aynı boyut ve kadraj**.
6. Hale/ateş halesi (alevli nimbus) yok: minyatürde peygamberlere ait bir semboldür. Işık gerekiyorsa "yumuşak ışık" denir.

## Stil cümlesi (her görsel komutunun sonuna)
```
Ottoman and Persian miniature painting style, flat vivid colors, fine gold detailing, delicate ink outlines, lapis blue, vermilion, turquoise and gold palette, elegant stylized proportions. Full body, standing, three-quarter view, centered, feet visible, same scale and framing as before. Flat solid magenta background (#E8087F), no pink or magenta on the character, no text, no letters, no calligraphy, no halo.
```

## Görünümler

**1 — Garip** (`karakter-01-garip`)
```
A humble young man at the very beginning of his journey: simple patched beige robe, plain cloth cap, worn sandals, a small cloth bundle on his shoulder, slim, no beard, modest and quiet posture.
```

Sonrakilerde başa ekle:
```
This is the same man at his previous stage. Create his NEXT stage: same face, same body, same pose style, same scale and framing, clearly the same person but grown.
```

```
**3 — Er**

Sturdier: padded cotton tunic with a leather belt, a small dagger at his waist, leather boots, a neater turban, steady confident stance.
```
**4 — Alp**
```
A young warrior: light chainmail under a red kaftan, a round shield on his back, a curved sword at his side, red leather boots, short dark beard.
```
**5 — Bahadır**
```
A brave hero: ornate lamellar armor with decorated shoulder plates, crimson kaftan, a bow and quiver on his back, a helmet wrapped with a turban and a small plume.

**6 — Ulu**
```
A noble figure: rich lapis-blue kaftan covered with golden floral patterns, a fur-trimmed cloak, a jeweled belt, a tall turban with a feather aigrette (sorguç), dignified posture.
```
**7 — Bilge**
```
A wise warrior-sage: deep green and gold layered robes, a leather scroll case at his side, a staff with an ornate brass head, full dark beard, calm thoughtful expression.
```

**9 — Nurlu**

Radiant: flowing white and gold robes, soft warm golden light emanating from his whole figure, small motes of light drifting around him, peaceful expression. No halo.

**10 — Simurg**

His final legendary form: robes patterned with the feathers of a great mythical bird, a long cloak shaped like spread wings of turquoise, emerald and gold feathers, a single majestic feather crest on his turban, gentle light around him, majestic yet humble.
```

## Animasyon (görselden video)
Her görünümün görselini yükle, şunu yaz:
```
Animate this character: subtle idle breathing, robe and cloak gently swaying, small natural movements. The character stays in exactly the same place and size. No camera movement, no zoom. Keep the flat solid magenta background unchanged, add no scenery. Seamless loop.
```
8–10. görünümlerde sonuna ekle: `The golden light and floating motes drift slowly.`

> Savaş için ileride ayrıca **saldırı** animasyonları gerekecek; şimdilik sadece bekleme yeterli.
